Case studies

Trace: an AI co-pilot for design decisions

2026 · Master thesis, KTH · Research + build
A task-aware AI co-pilot that helps designers think, not just generate. Expert interviews and a with/without-AI design experiment shaped a six-stage framework; I built a React + LLaMA prototype and iterated it through two usability studies with 12 participants.

Kallos: AI platform for employer-branding analysis

2024 · Bachelor thesis with PrideCom · UX + full-stack
Turned a consultancy’s manual employer-branding audits into a self-serve product: research, UX, a Flask backend, encrypted data and a local LLM. In two testing rounds with 11 participants, everyone completed the core tasks and 90% found the AI recommendations actionable.

Sällskap: group dining, coordinated

2026 · Personal project · UX + full-stack
A web app that collects a group’s availability and dietary needs privately, then recommends restaurants that fit everyone. Researched with 5 interviews and a 5-platform analysis, built with React and Supabase, iterated after 5 usability sessions.

Trivia Master: quiz app

In a four-person team I owned the experience, from information architecture and the Figma design system to the React Native screens. Every tester completed every core task in testing.
